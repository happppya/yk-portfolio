# Structural Requirements

Contract and content requirements for the site. This file states **what must exist
and what content it holds** — not how it is visually designed or implemented.

## Global

- The site has **four pages**: Me, Art, Music, Research.

---

## Me

- **"Yujin Kim"** at the top left.
- Under the name: a space for a **resume link**.
- On the right: space for a **portrait photo**, with **text underneath** it.

---

## Art

- The page shows **images of art pieces**.
- **On hover**: a **description widget** appears.
- **On click**: navigates to **more information**, giving:
  - title
  - year
  - material
  - description
  - a **close-up view** of the piece

---

## Music

- A **video plays automatically**, next to **text about experiences**.
- **Below**: photos and more videos, such as **"Bach prelude"**, **"viola"**, **"concerto"**.

---

## Research

Three sections:

1. **First section**
   - A space for **projects** with **images and descriptions**.
   - A **widget for a research paper**.

2. **Second section — titled "GHP"**
   - **Images** and some **text**.
   - A **widget for the paper** written there.

3. **Third section**
   - A section for **smaller projects**.
